Conghui Hu

I am a Lecturer at the School of Computing, National University of Singapore (NUS). Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Computer Vision and Robot Perception (CVRP) Lab at NUS, where I collaborated with Prof. Gim Hee Lee. I received my Ph.D. degree from the University of Surrey in 2021, advised by Prof. Yi-Zhe Song, Prof. Timothy Hospedales and Dr. Yongxin Yang. Prior to that, I obtained my Bachelor's Degree from the Sino-UK Joint Programme between Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications and Queen Mary University of London in 2016.
