Conghui Hu

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher in Computer Vision and Robot Perception(CVRP) Lab, School of Computing, National University of Singapore (NUS), where I collaborate with Prof. Gim Hee Lee. I received my Ph.D. degree from University of Surrey in 2021, advised by Prof. Yi-Zhe Song, Prof. Timothy Hospedales and Dr. Yongxin Yang. Prior to that, I obtained my Bachelor's Degree from the Sino-UK Joint Programme held between Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications and Queen Mary University of London in 2016.

My research interests revolve around computer vision and deep learning, with a particular focus on enhancing human interaction with the world through sketch-based applications, such as sketch-based image retrieval, sketch-guided object segmentation, and sketch-based 3D shape synthesis and editing.
